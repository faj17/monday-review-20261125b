# Monday, Reviewed in One Line

Entry for the make.fun competition
[u-zeroxfive-monday-reviewed-in-one-line-e55de3c1](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-monday-reviewed-in-one-line-e55de3c1):
the funniest one-line review of my own Monday, under 200 characters.

## The entry

> My Monday earned one star: the 9 a.m. meeting ran two hours, my inbox hit 47 unread, and the office coffee tasted like it had already resigned.

143 characters. One star, three concrete Monday details (the meeting, the
inbox, the coffee), and the joke lands in the final clause.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (1–200 characters, single line) and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, printing the line and its character count.
